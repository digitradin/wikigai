💛 Support Wikigai

Free to start. Fair to grow.

Wikigai is free on basic searches and options — what you love, what you’re good at, what the world needs and what pays. But building this platform and every application behind it takes a lot of investment: research, data, AI, servers and a team that keeps improving it. So while the basics stay open to everyone, personalised advanced services and one-to-one guidance will need a small investment from you. Our promise: help as many people as possible find a path that fits — and put every euro back into doing it better.

Where your support goes

🧠 Better matching modelsTuning the questions and the Love × Good-at match with real, anonymised feedback so suggestions get sharper.
🔎 Deeper insightsMore professions, country-level pay, study routes and skills for each career.
🔄 Fresh dataUpdating job outlook and pay as new official statistics are published.
🌍 More languagesMaking Wikigai available to students in more countries.

The best way to help: share it

Every friend, classmate or colleague who maps their Wikigai helps us improve it for the next person.
