2. What Wikigai is

Wikigai is an online career-exploration tool inspired by the ikigai idea: it helps you map what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs and what pays, suggests professions, and offers learning tools such as study plans (beta) and curated videos. It is available in English, French and Spanish. The basic features are free. You can support the project with an optional donation.
