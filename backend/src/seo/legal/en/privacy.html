The short version

  • We collect only what Wikigai needs to work — your email, name, password (stored as a one-way hash), your answers and results, and any optional details you choose to add.
  • No ads, no advertising cookies, no selling or renting of data. Our visitor statistics are cookieless and anonymous.
  • Your email and phone number are stored encrypted. AI features never receive your name, email or birthday.
  • You are in control: download or permanently delete all your data yourself at any time — even if you forgot your password, at wikigai.com/delete-my-data.

3. Cookies and local storage

Wikigai uses one strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in, and your browser’s local storage to remember preferences and progress on your device. We use no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to accept or refuse.

4. AI features (My learning › Plans)

To create profession overviews and study plans, we send AI model providers only the non-identifying information needed for the request (such as the profession and skills you chose, an age range, a country and the language) — never your name, email, phone, birthday or account identifiers, so a request cannot be linked back to you. AI answers can be wrong — please check them (see our Terms).

5. Videos (WikiVids)

Video searches are made by our server without any information about you. Videos play in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode; when you press play, YouTube receives your IP address and may store data on your device under Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Service providers

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run Wikigai and act on our instructions under a data processing agreement:

  • hosting and database provider (European Union);
  • email delivery provider (service emails only);
  • encrypted backup storage;
  • AI model providers — non-identifying requests only (section 4);
  • Google — YouTube videos and web fonts (Google receives your IP address when these load).

We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect the safety of users. The current list of providers is available on request.

7. International transfers

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some providers above may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States). In that case we rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Social media, TikTok sign-in and sharing

Signing in with TikTok

You can create a Wikigai account or sign in with “Continue with TikTok”, and connect or disconnect TikTok in Account › Sign-in methods. TikTok then gives us your TikTok account identifier, display name and profile picture — nothing else (no email, contacts, videos or messages). We use them only to sign you in to the same Wikigai account and to show which TikTok account is connected. The access token TikTok gives us at sign-in is revoked straight away. If you signed up with TikTok, we ask you once — you can skip it — to add your email, so you can recover your account.

Signing in with {{signin_apps}}

You can also create a Wikigai account or sign in with {{signin_apps}}, and connect or disconnect them in Account › Sign-in methods. The app gives us your account identifier, name and profile picture and, when it provides it, your verified email address — nothing else (no contacts, friends, posts, photos or messages). We use them only to sign you in to the same Wikigai account. If a Wikigai account already uses that email, we never join the two by ourselves: you sign in with your email and connect the app from your account. The access token the app gives us is used once to read these details and then revoked or discarded. What the app does with your data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Sharing to TikTok

If you choose “Share to TikTok”, TikTok asks you to authorise Wikigai to post on your behalf. On the post screen you see the account you are posting to, preview the images and choose the caption, who can view the post and the other settings. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you press Post. The images are created from your results, stored on our server only so TikTok can fetch them, and deleted within 24 hours. The access token is stored encrypted and deleted within 24 hours; we never keep a long-term token. We do not read your videos, followers or messages. You can disconnect TikTok at any time in Account › Sign-in methods or in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). What TikTok does with your data is governed by TikTok’s Privacy Policy.

Our own social media accounts

  • Wikigai publishes on its own accounts, including Instagram @wikigai_com and TikTok @wikigai.com, with a publishing tool that runs on our own server and connects only Wikigai’s brand accounts through the platforms’ official APIs. It does not collect data about our followers.
  • When you follow, like or comment on our accounts, the platform’s own privacy policy applies. We see only what the platform shows publicly (such as your username and comments) and use it only to reply and moderate.
  • Links in our posts carry a campaign tag (UTM) so our anonymous statistics can tell which post brought a visit — this never identifies you.
  • We do not offer sign-in with Facebook or Instagram and import no data from them.
  • When you sign in with Facebook or Instagram, we receive only the details listed above; we do not read your friends, followers, posts or messages and we never post for you.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a portable copy of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Most of this you can do yourself:

  • Correct — Account page (name, email, birthday, phone, countries, language).
  • Access and portability — Account page › “Download my data” gives you everything we hold about your account as a JSON file.
  • Delete — see section 10.

For anything else write to {{email}}. You can also complain to our lead supervisory authority, the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, aki.ee, info@aki.ee), or to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work (for example the AEPD in Spain or the CNIL in France).

10. How to delete your data

  1. Signed in: Account › “Delete my account”, confirm with your email and password.
  2. Forgot your password, or prefer not to sign in: go to wikigai.com/delete-my-data, enter your email, and confirm with the 6-digit code we send you.
  3. Signed up with TikTok and never added an email: sign in with TikTok, then Account › “Delete my account” › “Confirm with TikTok”.
  4. Signed up with {{signin_apps}} and never added an email: sign in with the same app, then Account › “Delete my account” › “Confirm with …”.
  5. By email: write from your account’s address to {{email}} with the subject “Delete my Wikigai data”. We act within one month and confirm by email.

Deletion is immediate and permanent for your account, profile, answers, results, plans, research, saved videos, pending codes, your sign-in app links (such as TikTok), access token and images. Encrypted backups roll over automatically and no longer contain your data after 30 days at most. Anonymous statistics are not linked to you and therefore stay. Comments you posted on our social media accounts belong to that platform — delete them there, or ask us and we will remove them from our pages.

11. Children and students

Wikigai is designed for students and adults. If you are under the age at which you can consent to online services in your country (13 in Estonia, 14 in Spain, 15 in France, 16 in many other EU countries, 13 in the UK), please use Wikigai only with the permission and involvement of a parent or guardian, who may contact us at any time to access or delete your data. Your birthday is optional and is used only to adapt the tone of study plans. If we learn that a child’s data was collected without the required permission, we delete it.

12. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive fields, passwords stored only in non-reversible form, restricted access, abuse protection and encrypted backups. No online service is risk-free: if a personal data breach occurs, we notify the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate within 72 hours and tell you directly when it is likely to put your rights at high risk, as the GDPR requires.
